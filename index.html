Mobile AI and Systems Lab

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Yonsei University

We aim to push the boundaries of what mobile and edge devices can deliver in everyday use. We bring intelligent, immersive, and interactive experiences to life through on-device and embodied AI, mobile immersive computing (AR/VR/XR), energy and thermal management, and cross-device computing.

We are actively seeking highly motivated Ph.D. and M.S. students, as well as undergraduate research interns. Please feel free to contact us!
